# Commit to ink before adding polygons

The visual state of the art in strategy games from 2019 to 2026 is defined by cohesion and hierarchy, not rendering fidelity: one material metaphor carried through every screen, a stylised map that owns the viewport and gets its beauty from cheap atmospheric layers, colour used as a code, characters shown large, battles that draw consequences on the board before playing them, and motion that is quick, proportional and skippable. Total War: Three Kingdoms (2019) remains the governing reference for this setting, and the 2023–2026 evidence (Crusader Kings III's Shan Shui paper map, the layered hero art of the 2026 三国志·战略版 refresh, Civilization VII's 21-month interface repair) sharpens that picture without overturning it. Against it, this game's weakness is direction and framing, not horsepower: its best art, 38 painted portraits, is shown at thumbnail size; its map sits in a card under half the screen and is split across three modes; and its interface speaks the language of a software dashboard. The biggest improvement comes from four phases in this order: commit to a single ink-on-paper language and put the map and portraits at full scale (CSS, layout and motion work, no new 3D); merge the three map modes into one WebGL map whose far zoom is an AI-painted ink-wash paper map with shader-drawn borders and cloud shadows; rebuild battle identity and impact with board-game techniques before adding polygons; then add depth through parallax portraits, decision previews and nested tooltips. The rendering advice is conservative: stay on three.js WebGLRenderer, share one renderer and one quality-tier system across map, duel and battles, and convert the 50 MB of JPG to AVIF. Evidence is strongest for the art-direction principles and the failure cases (developer interviews, official patch notes, engine documentation), moderate for map techniques, and weak for exact timings, result screens and any cost on integrated GPUs; every browser recipe below is an engineering inference not yet tested on this codebase. Avoid a WebGPU migration, heavyweight post-processing, rigged or 3D hero art that lags the paintings, map detail that outruns readability, and any animation a player cannot skip.

## Cohesion, not fidelity, defines the 2019–2026 state of the art

### One material metaphor runs through every screen

The most praised identity of the period comes from choosing one substrate and refusing to mix it. Creative Assembly's art director Pawel Wojs rejected the "skeuomorphic, wooden detailed panels" of earlier Total War games in favour of "lots of flowing black ink" on paper, with painted colour layered on top ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/how-creative-assembly-conceived-the-art-for-total-war-three-kingdoms/)), and the studio spent weeks filming real ink to build its shaders ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/total-war-three-kingdoms/total-war-three-kingdoms-features)). The detail critics remember is that **a screen which would elsewhere be an icon grid became one illustration that changes state**: the reform tree is an ink tree whose blossoms open as reforms unlock ([ifanr](https://www.ifanr.com/app/1218987)), prompting one reviewer to write "Very rare do I actually get to praise a menu for being lovely to look at" ([Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/review/total-war-three-kingdoms-review)). The same game ties hero classes, unit dress, building roofs and even its interface sound kits to the five Wu Xing colours, so hue works as a code and not as decoration ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/total-war-three-kingdoms/total-war-three-kingdoms-features); [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/audio/behind-the-audio-of-i-total-war-three-kingdoms-i-)).

Later titles repeat the pattern at different budgets. 三国望神州 (2025) frames every screen as a painting on silk with gongbi line art ([小米游戏中心](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1585024444_1761544204648_100)), and Romance of the Three Kingdoms 8 Remake (2024) shows a cheaper hybrid: officers and backdrops painted in 2D, with 3D reserved for shafts of light, cooking smoke and walking crowds ([Famitsu](https://www.famitsu.com/news/202309/21317855.html)). Both extremes are punished. Against the Storm's ornate interface had become "a patchwork of various ideas and styles"; its 2022 minimalist rewrite lost "the 'handcrafted', warm feeling", and the studio settled on texture plus clean structure ([Eremite Games](https://eremitegames.com/?p=236833)). Civilization VII's restrained launch interface was dismissed as "drab grey slabs" that looked "like an iPad version of Civ V" ([Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/civilization-vii-review/)).

### Maps are stylised dioramas lit by cheap atmosphere

Firaxis calls Civilization VII's style "readable realism" and names museum dioramas and painted game boards as its models ([TechRadar](https://techradar.com/gaming/consoles-pc/civilization-7-interview-preview-gamescom-art-direction)); Victoria 3's art brief is a map "interesting yet soothing enough not to compete in complexity with the interface" ([Paradox](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/victoria-3/news/dev-diary-49-graphic-overview)). Crusader Kings III's 2025 terrain rework states the scale rule outright (terrain is exaggerated "since real-world scale would look like noise when zoomed in") and adds drifting cloud shadows with an off switch, plus separate lighting for terrain and map objects so interactive things stand out ([CK3 Dev Diary 177](https://devtrackers.gg/crusader-kings/p/3c2f2816-dev-diary-177-a-fresh-coat-of-paint)). That update also introduced a second far-zoom paper map, the default for most East Asian cultures, whose terrain "draws on Chinese painting and Shan Shui, with bold strokes and leafy, steep mountains" ([CK3 Dev Diary 177](https://devtrackers.gg/crusader-kings/p/3c2f2816-dev-diary-177-a-fresh-coat-of-paint)). **This is the single most transferable idea in the research: the far-zoom layer can be a 2D illustration**, which is exactly what an AI-painting pipeline produces well.

None of this is asset-heavy. Songs of Conquest ships 128-pixel terrain textures and gets its look from 2D billboards on 3D ground, a projected lightmap that carries cloud shadows, and a post stack of bloom, vignette, colour grading and zoom-in depth of field ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/mixing-2d-billboards-and-3d-environments-in-a-game/)). Age of Wonders 4 covers unexplored land in animated mist with terrain silhouettes rendered into it ([Paradox](https://paradoxinteractive.com/games/age-of-wonders-4/news/age-of-wonders-4-dev-diary-7)), and Three Kingdoms adds distance fog, depth of field and a continuous day-night cycle to quieten distant noise ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/how-creative-assembly-conceived-the-art-for-total-war-three-kingdoms/)). The failure is just as consistent. Civilization VII players call its sprawling map "an overly-busy mess" while praising the terrain art itself ([CivFanatics](https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/civ-vii-map-graphics.699306/)); a Humankind playtester said "I feel like I am looking at a Magic Eye" ([Amplitude](https://community.amplitude-studios.com/amplitude-studios/humankind/forums/208-lucy-opendev/threads/38070-visually-overwhelming?page=1)); and Ara: History Untold, which launched with separate pretty and readable views, later added a merged mode with scaled-up units ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/?p=209905)).

### Interfaces keep the map dominant and make numbers explain themselves

Praised interfaces pin thin chrome to the screen edges and move detail into on-demand panels: the Crusader Kings III team avoids fullscreen menus so players keep "flicking between menus, the map and popups" ([Paradox](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/crusader-kings-iii/news/ck3-console-dev-diary-3-uiux-and-controls)), and Frostpunk 2's command radial blurs the scene and hides most of the HUD while open ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/09/18/adapting-frostpunk-2s-depth-to-a-gamepad/)). Nested, lockable tooltips with numeric breakdowns have been the genre standard since Crusader Kings III (2020), and Firaxis retrofitted them into Civilization VII in February 2026 ([2K](https://civilization.2k.com/civ-vii/game-update-notes/2026-feb-03-patch-1-3-2/)).

Civilization VII is the period's cautionary case. Released on 11 February 2025, it still sat at "Mixed" (48% positive) on Steam that May ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civilization_VII)), and in a 97-voter CivFanatics poll **functional failures (minimap 51.5%, city banner 40.2%, readability 35.1%) far outranked "more ornamentation" at 16.5%** ([CivFanatics](https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/which-ui-elements-do-you-think-need-work-the-most.694205/page-2)). One of the first fixes replaced the Simplified Chinese font ([2K](https://civilization.2k.com/civ-vii/game-update-notes/2025-mar-04-patch-1-1-0/)), a direct warning for a bilingual game. Motion evidence is thinner. General guidance puts most interface transitions at 100–400 ms with ease-out entries and observes that animations are far more often too long than too short ([Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-duration/)). Balatro is the reference for layered feedback scaled to the size of the event, although its widely quoted timings are a third party's reconstruction and not shipped values ([Blake Crosley](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/balatro)).

### Battles show consequences first and spectacle second

Into the Breach's designers "would sacrifice cool ideas for the sake of clarity every time" and found small animated tooltips "a thousand times more effective" than text ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/-i-into-the-breach-i-dev-on-ui-design-sacrifice-cool-ideas-for-the-sake-of-clarity-every-time-)). Triangle Strategy colours tiles as safe move, threatened move, attack range and area of effect, with red lines from each enemy that can strike the chosen tile ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Triangle-Strategy/archives/368789)), and a colour-blind reviewer's complaint that its blue and purple tiles were "indiscernible" shows the code must be doubled with pattern or shape ([RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/feature/project-triangle-strategys-invisible-feature-and-the-trials-of-gaming-while-colorblind/)). Battle Brothers, built by three people with one artist, replaced full-body figures with layered busts on plinths and deliberately oversized weapon icons, and cut walk animations altogether ([Battle Brothers dev blog](https://battlebrothersgame.com/?p=414)). **That is the most reusable token system for a project without animators.**

On spectacle, Fire Emblem Engage is praised because the top-down map "seamlessly" becomes the duel ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/reviews/fire-emblem-engage/)), and Unicorn Overlord's director concentrated animation effort on auto-resolved battles precisely because the player only watches them, while reviewers still prize the skip ([Inverse](https://nc.inverse.com/gaming/unicorn-overlord-interview-vanillaware); [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/unicorn-overlord)). A hugely popular XCOM 2 mod removes one-to-three-second pauses between actions and keeps the weapon animations ([Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/620600092)): players object to dead air, not to hits. Scale is now a shader problem, since a February 2026 three.js demonstration walks up to 100,000 figures in a single draw call ([three.js forum](https://discourse.threejs.org/t/one-draw-call-massive-crowd-performance-engineering-in-three-js/89928)). Both Three Kingdoms and Dynasty Warriors: Origins frame a duel by having soldiers form a ring around it ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2018/08/16/experiencing-the-duels-and-romance-of-total-war-three-kingdoms/); [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=396742)).

### Characters carry the fiction, and the face comes first

Koei Tecmo's artists state the house rule plainly: for a character "the face is everything", backgrounds exist to set off the face, portraits moved to waist-up framing from Romance of the Three Kingdoms XII, two instalments shipped a calm and a battle image for each officer, and some officers were redrawn about twenty times until the character came through ([4Gamer](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/)). Static art is accepted when excellent but no longer sufficient at full price: one review of the 2024 remake called its artwork "top notch" yet marked the presentation down for "static portraits" and found the 3D duel models "easily the weakest" element ([3rd-strike](https://3rd-strike.com/?p=169327)). The 2026 refresh of 三国志·战略版 shows the inexpensive answer: text, hero and background split into layers, with an animated environment per faction (mist and birds for Shu, sunlight on the Yangtze for Wu, snow for Wei) behind the figure ([小米游戏中心](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1279764826_1787911389955_16)). Type follows the same restraint. Black Myth: Wukong confines its custom running-script face to chapter and boss title cards ([Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/64872/black-myth-wukong-video-game-1)), and NetEase's interface team warns that calligraphic lettering must stay legible and be matched to its content ([机核](https://www.gcores.com/articles/153626)).

### In the browser, the admired work is stylised and still WebGL

The three.js manual still labels WebGPURenderer "experimental" and calls WebGLRenderer "the recommended choice for pure WebGL 2 applications" ([three.js manual](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/webgpurenderer.html)); a May 2026 benchmark of 4,000 meshes measured 4.5 ms of CPU per frame on WebGLRenderer against 9.7–10.3 ms on WebGPURenderer ([three.js forum](https://discourse.threejs.org/t/webgpurenderer-2x-slower-cpu-and-5-10x-slower-first-frame-than-webglrenderer-on-many-mesh-scenes-r183-same-on-both-backends/91904)). WebGLRenderer has meanwhile gained a built-in HDR effects path and cheaper bloom ([three.js PR #32461](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/pull/32461); [r186 notes](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/releases/tag/r186)). The celebrated browser pieces of 2025–2026 commit to one stylisation (Susurrus uses a single simplified Kuwahara filter as its only post-processing pass) ([Tproger](https://tproger.ru/translations/webgl-portfolio-susurrus-akvarelnyj-3d-mir-na-three-js-i-kuwah)), and none of the 33 works tagged as games in the webgpu.com showcase is a strategy title ([webgpu.com](https://webgpu.com/tags/game/)). **No visual bar exists yet for browser grand strategy**, which is an opening as much as a gap.

## The game's gap is direction and framing, not rendering power

Read against those references, the audit is a list of inverted priorities, not of missing technology. The duel proves the project can already light, grade and stage a 3D scene, yet nothing else inherits that pipeline, so quality swings from scene to scene in the way reviewers noticed when Romance of the Three Kingdoms 8 Remake set fine paintings beside weaker 3D ([3rd-strike](https://3rd-strike.com/?p=169327)). The map, the screen players look at longest, has under half the viewport, a permanent side panel taking about 30% of the width, and three modes dividing the effort: the reverse of the edge-chrome pattern, and the same split Ara abandoned. The navy glass, neon outlines and pill badges give a software-product look of the kind players rejected in Civilization VII, whose windows one player compared to "some minimally configured linux distro" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1295660/discussions/2/591762563949163578/)); three themes that differ only by accent colour add a testing burden without adding identity.

The same inversion holds for characters and battles. The portraits appear at 28–48 px, which defeats the face-first rule that makes Koei's rosters iconic, and the faction-select screen spends its space on stat tiles where genre peers put painted leaders. The tactical board repeats one image per terrain, so it reads as wallpaper and not as ground; its tokens are small circles; half the screen is text. The war scene is a modal 45% of the screen wide, filled with glyphs and dots, in the slot where Unicorn Overlord puts its best animation. Motion is absent, not wrong. Every one of these is a decision about direction, layout or scale, which is why the plan front-loads work that costs CSS and shader lines instead of assets.

### Phase 1: set one direction and put the art at scale

Phase 1 changes what every screenshot looks like without touching 3D. Replace glass and neon with three materials only: paper-toned reading surfaces, ink-dark frames whose edges are roughened shapes and not boxes, and a single gold accent, with a vermilion seal as punctuation for confirmations, captures and treaties. Creative Assembly describes exactly this substitution, and a Chinese review praised the result as "不流于表面的 UI 设计", design that is not skin-deep ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/how-creative-assembly-conceived-the-art-for-total-war-three-kingdoms/); [ifanr](https://www.ifanr.com/app/1218987)). In CSS this is a set of custom properties, an SVG turbulence filter or brush-stroke mask for panel edges, and a tiled grain texture; those recipes are the researchers' approximations, not sourced implementations. Keep faction colour as a separate accent channel. Koei's artists credit Dynasty Warriors with fixing Wei blue, Shu green and Wu red, and one recalls being told to paint Liu Bei in a red robe with only the innermost garment green ([4Gamer](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/index_2.html)): faction colour works as trim, not as a flood. Keep numbers on plain paper in dark ink so the style never costs contrast. **Collapse the three themes into this one.** The counter-argument is that players like choice, and Crusader Kings III does let players pick a paper-map style ([CK3 Dev Diary 177](https://devtrackers.gg/crusader-kings/p/3c2f2816-dev-diary-177-a-fresh-coat-of-paint)); but that is a choice between two fully realised looks, not three accent swaps, so a second theme should return only as a different material.

Then give the map the viewport: a thin top strip of whole numbers, small corner clusters, and the permanent side panel demoted to a contextual sheet for the selected city, army or officer. Victoria 3 hides top-bar items that do not apply and cut its notifications by "roughly 50%" in one patch ([Paradox](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/victoria-3/news/dev-diary-74-ux-improvements)); Civilization VII rounded its top-bar yields after complaints ([2K](https://civilization.2k.com/civ-vii/game-update-notes/2025-apr-22-patch-1-2-0/)). No source measured how much screen leading games leave free, so this rests on qualitative developer and reviewer testimony.

The cheapest large gain is scale for the existing portraits. Rebuild faction select as three full-height painted banners with the leader dominant (Firaxis's refreshed loading screen put "leader portraits front and center") ([2K](https://civilization.2k.com/civ-vii/game-update-notes/2025-jun-23-patch-1-2-2/)), and give every officer a sheet with a waist-up crop over a slow animated faction scene. Recruitment reveals follow the 战略版 order of faction, figure, text, then attributes ([小米游戏中心](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1279764826_1787911389955_16)). Lists keep tight face crops, as Koei's framing history suggests.

Type and motion complete the phase. Reserve brush calligraphy for names, screen titles and single-character stamps, set narrative text in a Song-style serif and data in a sans with tabular numerals; guidance for Chinese text recommends leading of 120–145% and warns against automatic font shrinking ([Culturalization guide](https://sites.google.com/view/culturalizationguide/culture-taboos/user-interface/font-issues)). Define motion tokens once. The researchers suggest roughly 80–170 ms for press and hover and 220–300 ms for panels and modals, with exits faster than entries; these ranges are derived from general interface guidance such as Microsoft's 83, 167 and 250 ms control durations, not measured from any game ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/motion/timing-and-easing)). Then add resource count-ups, gain-and-loss fly-outs, a seal-stamp confirmation and one global motion-scale variable that honours the browser's reduced-motion preference. Three Kingdoms' audio team kept interface sound "clean, minimal and refined" to match the ink ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/audio/behind-the-audio-of-i-total-war-three-kingdoms-i-)); one dry thud synchronised to the stamp follows that lead. Finally, convert the JPGs to AVIF with a fallback and generate thumbnail sizes. AVIF has **96.71% browser support** ([caniuse](https://caniuse.com/avif)), though the size saving on painterly images is an unmeasured estimate.

### Phase 2: merge three maps into one living ink map

Start with the plumbing the duel already hints at: one WebGLRenderer shared by map, duel and battle scenes; a three-tier quality setting chosen by a short warm-up benchmark and adjusted with hysteresis, the pattern drei's PerformanceMonitor documents ([drei](https://drei.docs.pmnd.rs/performances/performance-monitor)); a pixel-ratio cap; bloom at half resolution; grade, vignette and paper grain folded into one fullscreen pass; and `compileAsync()` behind every scene transition to prevent shader stalls ([three.js docs](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGLRenderer.html)). A published rule of thumb says half-resolution post-processing "can roughly double frame rate" when fill rate is the limit ([Utsubo](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/threejs-best-practices-100-tips)), which is the likely cause of the duel's 36 fps; this must be measured, because no source gives costs for integrated GPUs. Paintings used as WebGL textures need their own budget: a 4096-pixel-square texture occupies about 90 MB of GPU memory whatever its file size, and KTX2 compression cuts that four- to eight-fold ([Don McCurdy](https://www.donmccurdy.com/2024/02/11/web-texture-formats/)). Prefer Neutral or AgX tone mapping to ACES so faction colours keep their hue, since Khronos notes that ACES washes out bright yellows, greens and cyans ([Khronos](https://www.khronos.org/news/press/khronos-pbr-neutral-tone-mapper-released-for-true-to-life-color-rendering-of-3d-products)).

Then retire the CSS-tilted SVG as the default and make the WebGL map the map, keeping the flat SVG only as a fallback for machines without usable WebGL. Drive a single "paperness" value from camera height: near the ground, a displaced heightmap with exaggerated relief; far out, a cross-fade to one large AI-painted ink-wash map of China with brush mountains and seal-style city marks, aligned to the terrain. Pair the zoom with explicit map-mode buttons, because Crusader Kings III players called zoom-only switching "needlessly constraining" ([Paradox forum](https://forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/threads/ckiii-dev-diary-25-map-features-and-map-modes.1388210/)), and cross-fade across a height band, because pop-in between layers was a long-standing complaint that the 2025 update finally removed ([CK3 Dev Diary 177](https://devtrackers.gg/crusader-kings/p/3c2f2816-dev-diary-177-a-fresh-coat-of-paint)). Replace the starfield with a lacquer table or paper margin; Victoria 3 frames its paper map inside a table scene so the player feels they are "overlooking the world as a whole" ([Paradox](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/victoria-3/news/dev-diary-49-graphic-overview)).

Ownership becomes a data texture. Europa Universalis V stores one unique colour per location in an ID image ([EU5 wiki](https://eu5.paradoxwikis.com/Map_modding)), and a browser reimplementation of the EU4 map draws borders by comparing neighbouring pixels in **about 600 lines of GLSL at roughly 0.1 ms per redraw** ([nickb.dev](https://nickb.dev/blog/simulating-the-eu4-map-in-the-browser-with-webgl)). With a few dozen territories this is small, and it turns conquest into a reward: animate the new owner's colour soaking outward from the captured city. Romance of the Three Kingdoms XIV built its identity on painting the map in faction colour, and a reviewer found it dull only when it amounted to "armies painting in the blanks" ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/romance-of-the-three-kingdoms-14/review)). Add the three atmosphere layers that carry most of the mood for the least code (a scrolling noise texture for cloud shadows, distance fog, one colour grade) and offer tilt-shift as an optional high-tier extra. Build relief from open elevation data: Terrain Tiles on AWS are listable without an account, and Natural Earth's river and coast vectors are public domain ([AWS](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/); [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)). Render rivers and coastline from distance-field masks so they read as ink strokes and not as thin lines.

Cities and armies finish the phase. Use four or five discrete city tiers assembled from a small procedural kit, with faction banners and night lanterns, in the manner of Victoria 3's hubs that grow as buildings pass thresholds ([Paradox forum](https://forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/threads/victoria-3-dev-diary-50-living-map.1531318/)); keep footprints tight and labels large. Replace the dashed line and beacon with a general-on-horse miniature carrying a tall banner (in Three Kingdoms a general "can be seen walking around with his army") ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=320428)), reusing the duel's procedural rig and enlarging it with camera distance, since Ara's fix was units "scaled on the map to enhance visibility" ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/?p=209905)). Unseen land becomes bare paper or ink mist with faint mountain silhouettes, after Age of Wonders 4 ([Paradox](https://paradoxinteractive.com/games/age-of-wonders-4/news/age-of-wonders-4-dev-diary-7)).

### Phase 3: give battles a board, weight and a finale

Keep the 8×8 grid in the DOM at one fixed angle and change what it shows. Lay each battle on a single painted battlefield generated per terrain set, with the grid drawn over it, so tiles join into a landscape. Fixed boards also sidestep the cost Triangle Strategy's developers describe, where "making maps observable from every side took a lot of resources" ([Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/triangle-strategy-devs-on-how-the-game-uses-accurate-hd-2d)). Replace round thumbnails with the Battle Brothers token: a portrait bust on a faction-coloured plinth, an oversized troop-type glyph, and a small banner whose state carries morale, a Total War convention ([Battle Brothers dev blog](https://battlebrothersgame.com/?p=414); [Total War manual](https://r2enc.totalwar.com/en/manual/single-player/0087_enc_page_battle_play_phase_conflict_morale)). Adopt the four tile states with a pattern or outline on each, draw threat lines from enemies to the hovered tile, ghost the unit at its destination with the predicted health change, and show turn order as a strip that updates on hover, the change reviewers singled out in the 2025 Final Fantasy Tactics remaster ([RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/preview/18317-final-fantasy-tactics-the-ivalice-chronicles-new-voice-acting-makes-it-feel-like-a-whole-new-game)). Let the board take most of the screen and move the text dashboard into a contextual strip.

Impact is timing, not animation. The documented toolkit is anticipation, a 60–120 ms freeze on contact, shake scaled to damage and driven by smooth noise, a health bar that highlights the loss and then drains, and a brief light under every effect ([game-feel guide](https://gamedo-live.pages.dev/news/game-feel-juice-guide-indie-devs); [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1023146/Math-for-Game-Programmers-Juicing); [Total War](https://www.totalwar.com/?p=16273); [Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/spotlights/octopath-traveler-s-hd-2d-art-style-and-story-make-for-a-jrpg-dream-come-true)). These are summary-level sources, but they agree with each other and are cheap to try. Ship speed settings with more than one step, a skip for every scene, and separate controls for shake and flashes, as Xbox Accessibility Guideline 117 asks ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/117)).

The auto-resolved war scene should become the full-screen showpiece. Seed its backdrop from the terrain being fought over, the continuity Fire Emblem Engage is praised for; build armies from parallax layers of sprite crowds, or from one instanced soldier mesh animated in the vertex shader if the 2D version reads thin; and show casualties by thinning ranks and dropping banners. The 2D layering recipe is the researchers' inference from adjacent evidence, not a documented technique. End on a verdict with no dead air (a brushed 勝 or 敗 seal, then a casualty ledger that counts up), because Teamfight Tactics found that a short ceremony "made Top Four feel more like a win" ([Riot](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-tft-ko-coliseum-learnings)) and reviewers noticed the pause before Advance Wars registered a victory ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-advance-wars-12-re-boot-camp-isnt-here-to-make-friends/)). For the duel, add the ring of watching soldiers and a two-portrait cut-in before the fight; both frame the existing high-water mark at little cost.

### Phase 4: add depth where players linger

Once the language holds everywhere, deepen it. Generate a depth map for each portrait and backdrop with an Apache-licensed estimator such as Depth Anything V2-Small ([GitHub](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2)) and add pointer-driven parallax with slow idle drift; generate a second, fiercer pose for lead officers to use in duels and cut-ins, as Koei did. The parallax shader is untested here, and the researchers expect artefacts at depth edges when motion is strong. Rebuild order menus as sheets that preview consequences before commitment (officer portraits, before-and-after numbers, the march drawn on the map), following Endless Legend 2, whose previews let players "always make good decisions in the moment" ([DBLTAP](https://www.dbltap.com/reviews/endless-legend-2-early-access-review)), and Civilization VII's redesigned production menu ([2K](https://civilization.2k.com/civ-vii/game-update-notes/2025-sep-30-patch-1-2-5/)). Add nested tooltips with timer or action locks and numeric breakdowns, the Crusader Kings III model ([Obsidian forum, quoting the dev diary](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/nested-popovers/573)). Turn the technology screen into one ink illustration that blooms as research completes. On the high quality tier only, trial a half-resolution Kuwahara pass in the duel to pull procedural geometry toward the painted portraits ([Maxime Heckel](https://blog.maximeheckel.com/posts/on-crafting-painterly-shaders)).

## What to avoid: traps other games already paid for

The first trap is technological ambition. **Do not migrate to WebGPURenderer now.** It is officially experimental; every `ShaderMaterial` and EffectComposer pass would need rewriting; its WebGL fallback is the same new renderer and not a route back; and a high-priority issue on per-object uniform buffers was still open in September 2026 ([three.js manual](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/webgpurenderer.html); [three.js issue #30560](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/issues/30560)). Agency blogs call WebGPU "production-ready"; the maintainers' manual does not ([Utsubo](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/webgpu-threejs-migration-guide)). Skip volumetric clouds, whose own documentation says "Clouds are expensive" ([three-clouds](https://github.com/takram-design-engineering/three-geospatial/tree/main/packages/clouds)), along with screen-space reflections, screen-space global illumination and anisotropic Kuwahara. Skip cascaded shadows too: the SunLight documentation says it is "not a good fit for small, bounded scenes like dioramas" ([three.js docs](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/SunLight.html)). Do not stack fullscreen passes at full pixel ratio on integrated GPUs.

The second trap is art the pipeline cannot sustain. Rigged 2D portraits need illustrations split into layers with hidden areas repainted by hand, and Spine requires a paid editor ([CSDN](https://gitcode.csdn.net/69bc8e170a2f6a37c598b9b8.html); [Esoteric Software](https://esotericsoftware.com/spine-purchase)). 3D officer models that trail the paintings get noticed, as the remake's did. Guard the AI-generated roster against same-face syndrome: a Chinese reviewer faulted Three Kingdoms because many famous figures share "同一张龙套脸", one generic extra's face ([少数派](https://sspai.com/post/55115)). On the map, avoid busy high-contrast terrain textures (Humankind's "too many high-contrast details") ([Amplitude](https://community.amplitude-studios.com/amplitude-studios/humankind/forums/208-lucy-opendev/threads/38070-visually-overwhelming?page=1)), city sprawl that buries geography, realistic unit scale and a rotating battle camera. Do not mix materials (wood, bronze, glass and neon in one screen is what Creative Assembly left behind), and do not over-correct into flat slabs, the lesson of both Civilization VII and Against the Storm.

The third trap is presentation that outstays its welcome. Keep calligraphy out of body text: Persona 5's stylised type became "a readability nightmare" in localisation ([Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/persona-5-tactica-user-interface)). Avoid saturated red text on dark surfaces, a specific Civilization VII complaint ([CivFanatics](https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/which-ui-elements-do-you-think-need-work-the-most.694205/page-2)). Never carry a tile state by colour alone. Make every long animation skippable, and give all motion an off switch; Balatro players with motion sickness disable shake and still struggle with the moving background ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4293690852338460469)). Never let a pop-up land on top of a pending choice, as Endless Legend 2 does ([DBLTAP](https://www.dbltap.com/reviews/endless-legend-2-early-access-review)). Keep animated texture away from text; Against the Storm moved its animated background behind the icon instead ([Eremite Games](https://eremitegames.com/interface-hotfix-we-hear-you/)).

## How far the evidence reaches

Three tiers of evidence sit under this plan. The strongest is first-hand and consistent: Creative Assembly's art-direction interviews, the 4Gamer conversation with Koei Tecmo's portrait artists, Paradox's Crusader Kings III and Victoria 3 art diaries, the Songs of Conquest technical breakdown, the Battle Brothers and Into the Breach developer accounts, Eremite's devlogs, Firaxis's patch notes and the three.js manual. These support the direction (one material, map first, face first, clarity first, stay on WebGL) with confidence. The middle tier is review and reception evidence, which shows what gets praised and criticised but is self-selected; the Civilization VII map complaints, for example, come from forum threads, and no professional essay or usability metric backs them. The weakest tier is search-summary evidence the researchers could not open (most Paradox forum diaries, the Fire Emblem and Triangle Strategy tile conventions, the hit-stop and shake figures) together with third-party reconstructions such as the Balatro timings.

Several things are simply unknown. No source measured how much of the screen leading games leave to the map, because the screenshot databases were inaccessible. No source gives frame-time costs for bloom, depth of field or Kuwahara filtering on integrated GPUs, so every post effect in this plan needs a measurement on the target machine before it ships. No published three.js implementation of an ink-wash or parchment map shader was found, and one September 2026 case study on the subject could not be read ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/09/09/still-from-akira-to-ink-wash-building-a-generative-garden-in-webgpu/)). Result and aftermath screens are the thinnest-sourced topic of all. How Three Kingdoms itself draws fog of war and map edges could not be verified. The evidence on recent Chinese titles comes from single reviews, some of them close to marketing. No telemetry on skip rates, animation length or map readability was found for any game. And the implementation recipes, the payoff and effort ratings and the phase order are inferences by the researchers and this report: informed, mutually consistent and untested.

## Conclusion

The research changes the question. The project has been raising its ceiling one scene at a time, and the duel is the proof, while the genre's evidence says players judge the floor: whether every screen speaks one language, and whether the thing they stare at longest deserves the stare. The duel should stop being the model for the rest of the game and become a tenant of it, with its renderer, grade and camera craft shared and its 3D ambition left where it is.

The constraint that looked most limiting, having no artists, turns out to choose the right direction. Every route that leans on 2D illustration (a painted paper map, painted battlefields, layered portraits, ink transitions) is one an AI painting pipeline can feed and one the best references already use; every route that leans on bespoke 3D or rigging exposes the gap. Because no celebrated browser grand-strategy game exists, a cohesive ink-wash Three Kingdoms map with living borders would not be catching up to a standard; it would be setting one. The order in which Firaxis repaired Civilization VII is the sequencing rule to carry through all four phases: fix alignment, contrast and legibility first, surface the numbers second, add ornament last, and measure on the integrated GPU before keeping any effect.
