# Art direction and character presentation in recent Three Kingdoms and East-Asian historical strategy games (2019–2026)

Researcher notes, compiled 23 November 2026. Release years are given in brackets after each game. "Snippet-level" means the fact came from a search-result summary and the page itself could not be opened (rate-limited, paywalled or truncated); treat those as weaker evidence. "Browser approximation" bullets under Inferences are my own implementation suggestions for a solo developer using React, Tailwind, three.js and AI-painted 2D art; they are not sourced claims.

Games referenced: Total War: Three Kingdoms (TW3K, 2019, Creative Assembly); Romance of the Three Kingdoms XIV (RTK14, 2020, Koei Tecmo); Romance of the Three Kingdoms 8 Remake (RTK8R, Oct 2024; Power-Up Kit edition 29 Jan 2026); Nobunaga's Ambition: Awakening (Japan 2022, West 2023, Complete Edition 2025); Dynasty Warriors: Origins (DWO, Jan 2025); 三国志·战略版 (2019, 3D refresh covered in 2020 and 2026 sources); 率土之滨 (2015, ongoing redraws); 鸿图之下 (2020); 三国：谋定天下 (2024); 三国望神州 / Shenzhou Saga: Three Kingdoms (2025); 三国志：王道天下 (2026); 三国志·汉末霸业 / Three Kingdoms: The Last Warlord (2017 onward); Black Myth: Wukong (2024, art reference only).

## 1. Visual identity: which art styles are used, and how are they applied consistently across map, UI, portraits and battle scenes?

### Takeaway
The strongest identities come from choosing one material metaphor and running it through everything: TW3K (2019) replaced wooden skeuomorphic panels with "flowing black ink" on paper and layered painterly colour on top, while 三国望神州 (2025) frames every screen as a silk scroll with gongbi and baimiao line art. Koei Tecmo's 2024 remake uses a cheaper but effective hybrid: 2D painted officers and backdrops, with 3D used only for light, smoke, crowds and ink-painted distant landscapes.

### Cited Findings

**Total War: Three Kingdoms (2019)**
- Art director Pawel Wojs said the team rejected the wooden, skeuomorphic panels of earlier Total War games: "we wanted to take a much more minimalist approach." The base is "lots of flowing black ink you can see in the UI, with this paper just dipped in the ink", and detail is layered over that ink base "to give you the character illustrations", producing "this painterly effect." — [GamesBeat, Aug 2018](https://gamesbeat.com/how-creative-assembly-conceived-the-art-for-total-war-three-kingdoms/)
- Wojs again: "we moved away from the skeumorphic panels of the UI to something much more freeform, with flowing ink." Senior designer Leif Walter: "The art style is very much inspired by a modern painter painting these historical landscapes" and "The look we try to have is a very modern take on the historical material." — [TheSixthAxis interview, 16 Aug 2018](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2018/08/16/interview-exploring-ancient-china-in-total-war-three-kingdoms/)
- Wojs described the target as "this ethereal, mystical depiction of China" seen "through a modern lens", with "color, lots of color" because the period is "a period of hope." — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/how-creative-assembly-conceived-the-art-for-total-war-three-kingdoms/)
- Campaign map: detail is layered so that "as you get closer you get more information, more detail"; distant objects are simplified and the team added "fog and depth of field" to cut visual noise; a continuous day and night cycle runs on the map. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/how-creative-assembly-conceived-the-art-for-total-war-three-kingdoms/)
- Night battles were lit to be "mystical, almost magical" and "not too dark and gritty", "which is why it's so blue", with lanterns and fires providing warm contrast. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/how-creative-assembly-conceived-the-art-for-total-war-three-kingdoms/)
- The reform (technology) screen is drawn as an ink plum-blossom tree; blossoms open on the matching branch when a reform is unlocked ("以水墨梅花的形式展现"). The main menu carries a crimson seal carved with 「三国」. — [爱范儿 ifanr, 12 Jun 2019](https://www.ifanr.com/app/1218987)
- A Western reviewer on the same screen: the Reforms tree is "quite literally, in the shape of a tree" and "It's bloody beautiful"; "Very rare do I actually get to praise a menu for being lovely to look at." — [Wccftech, 16 May 2019](https://wccftech.com/review/total-war-three-kingdoms-review)
- The opening cinematic renders the burning of Luoyang in ink style ("以水墨风格表现出的洛阳兵燹"). — [ifanr](https://www.ifanr.com/app/1218987)
- Trailer studio Platige chose ink and calligraphy as "the most expressive and the most plastic theme" for early China and reused a "spilling ink" technique from its earlier TW3K cinematic, re-tinted yellow for the Yellow Turban trailer and adapted to an accelerating camera. — [Platige](https://platige.com/?p=8499)
- Senior designer Dominique Starr said the visuals were finished "with a slightly modern Chinese art twist"; writer Pete Stewart said the team drew on contemporary images of China for the maps. — [History Hit, 1 Oct 2021](https://www.historyhit.com/gaming/total-war-three-kingdoms-chinese-history/)

**Koei Tecmo (RTK14 2020, RTK8 Remake 2024)**
- RTK8R is described by development producer Hisatsugu Ishikawa as a "2Dと3Dのハイブリッド": officers and backgrounds are painted in 2D, and 3D is used to add shafts of light, cooking smoke rising from towns and people walking the roads. — [Famitsu, 21 Sep 2023](https://www.famitsu.com/news/202309/21317855.html)
- Producer Kazuhiro Echigoya: moving to a city screen shows "山水画のような背景" (a landscape-painting-like background); Ishikawa: more people walk the streets as a city's population grows. — [Famitsu, 21 Sep 2023](https://www.famitsu.com/news/202309/21317855.html)
- Battle maps went "from a single 2D picture to a mix of 2D and 3D" and "distant landscapes are rendered as ink paintings." — [Gematsu, Oct 2023](https://www.gematsu.com/2023/10/romance-of-the-three-kingdoms-8-remake-details-new-features-enhancements)
- RTK14's identity is the single map that players "paint" in faction colour down to the hexagon; a review describes the map as a canvas with units painting streaks across it. — [Twinfinite, 2019](https://twinfinite.net/news/romance-of-the-three-kingdoms-xiv-map-video/); [TheSixthAxis review, 4 Mar 2020](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2020/03/04/romance-of-the-three-kingdoms-xiv-review-ps4/) (both snippet-level)
- RTK14 director Toshiyuki Kobayashi: "By having the entire battlefield encompassed by one map, players can create their own strategies." Cover illustrator Keiji Hida: "I wanted to go back to the visual elements of the three rulers from the first ROTK game cover." — [Koei Tecmo Europe creator page](https://koeitecmoeurope.com/rtk14/creator.html)

**Dynasty Warriors: Origins (2025), art direction only**
- Director Kazutoshi Sekiguchi said the first concept art showed huge armies colliding under a rain of arrows, and that this image drove the "臨場感溢れる戦場" (battlefield with presence) goal and details such as drifting sparks. The tone moved toward "歴史ドラマのような雰囲気" (historical drama), so "キャラクターデザインも少し渋めなものになっています" (character designs are a little more subdued). — [GAME Watch, 27 Sep 2024](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/interview/1624598.html)

**Chinese-developed titles**
- 三国望神州 (2025, 杭州弹指宇宙): "水墨工笔" (ink-wash plus fine-line gongbi) with pale colour; scenes read as an unrolling landscape scroll; UI is "画幅化、丝帛化" (framed as paintings on silk) with classical patterns; 小人书 (old picture-book) style illustrations are inserted between screens; upgrade, gacha and training screens use baimiao (plain line-drawing) figures. — [小米游戏中心 review, 27 Oct 2025](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1585024444_1761544204648_100)
- 三国志·汉末霸业 (2017 onward): portraits use the baimiao "绣像" look of classic Romance of the Three Kingdoms 连环画 with colour added, and the interface is antique-styled. — [游民星空](https://www.gamersky.com/handbook/201705/909929.shtml) (snippet-level)
- 三国志·战略版 (2019): moved from 2D to 3D with seasons, day and night, wind direction, and particle effects such as dust from hooves and spray from warships. — [TapTap article, modified 20 Oct 2020](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/67638305742652786)
- 鸿图之下 (2020, UE4): the publisher-side producer said UE4 was chosen to improve first visual impression and to show historical figures as characters rather than only as cards; generals were motion-captured. — [小米游戏中心 interview](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1273179761_1599793310471_16); [review](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1312738380_1606567678068_16) (snippet-level)
- 三国志：王道天下 (2026, Koei Tecmo licence): marketed as a "次世代3D美术焕新" on top of the classic RTK look, with PBR rendering and 3D generals. The only source found is a promotional user submission. — [机核, 18 Mar 2026](https://www.gcores.com/articles/212140)

**Black Myth: Wukong (2024), reference only**
- Each chapter ends with an animated short in a different technique (2D hand-drawn, stop-motion, ink-wash and others). — [机核 / 动画学术趴, 22 Aug 2024](https://www.gcores.com/articles/187061)

### Inferences
- The common pattern in the praised games is one substrate plus one accent: paper and black ink plus saturated colour (TW3K); silk scroll plus pale gongbi colour (望神州); painted 2D plus sparing 3D atmosphere (RTK8R). Mixed metaphors (wood, metal, parchment and neon in one screen) are what the TW3K team explicitly moved away from.
- TW3K treats UI panels as shapes of ink on paper, with no boxes or bevels. Screens that would be a grid of icons elsewhere (the reform tree) become a single illustration that changes state. That is cheap to imitate and is the detail reviewers remember.
- Browser approximation, ink panels: build panels from a paper-coloured base with an ink-shaped mask. Use an SVG filter (`feTurbulence` feeding `feDisplacementMap`) on a rounded rectangle to roughen edges, or a pre-made brush-stroke alpha PNG applied with CSS `mask-image` or 9-slice `border-image`. Add paper grain with a tiled noise texture and `mix-blend-mode: multiply`. All achievable by one developer.
- Browser approximation, ink transitions: a full-screen fragment shader (three.js quad or a 2D canvas) that thresholds fractal noise combined with a radial distance field, animating the threshold, gives an ink-bleed wipe. A cheaper variant is a luminance-mask sprite sheet or short video used as `mask-image`.
- Browser approximation, map: in the three.js map shader, blend unexplored or distant regions toward a desaturated paper tone with darker ink edges (noise-perturbed boundary), and fade map edges into paper. Add distance fog and a mild depth-of-field or tilt-shift pass, as TW3K's team describes for noise reduction. Faction territory can be shown as translucent colour wash, in the spirit of RTK14's map painting.
- Browser approximation, state-changing illustration: an SVG tree whose branches draw in with `stroke-dashoffset` and whose blossoms scale and fade in on unlock reproduces the TW3K reform tree idea without any new painted art.
- Browser approximation, RTK8R hybrid: keep painted backdrops and add only cheap motion on top (canvas particles for smoke and mist, CSS-animated light shafts, a few sprite walkers). This matches the existing asset base (painted battlefield and faction backdrops).
- Needs professionals: bespoke hand-painted ink cinematics like Platige's trailers, and frame-by-frame animated shorts like Black Myth's chapter endings. AI-painted stills with pan, zoom and ink wipes are the realistic substitute.

### Gaps
- I could not find a primary Creative Assembly source describing how TW3K renders fog of war, map edges or loading screens. The official dev diaries on totalwar.com returned HTTP 429 on every attempt, and no ArtStation or GDC breakdown by the UI artists surfaced. My unverified recollection is that unexplored territory appears as a paper-and-ink drawn map and that loading screens are ink paintings with quotations; this should be checked against screenshots.
- Game UI Database and Interface In Game pages for TW3K could not be located (one guessed URL returned 404).
- No art-direction interview was found for 三国：谋定天下, 率土之滨 or 三国志·战略版; available material is player commentary or promotional copy. 三国群英传8 and 吞食孔明传 art coverage was not found before the session's search budget ran out.
- Nobunaga's Ambition: Awakening art direction (map, castles) had no developer source; only review comments (see section 7).

## 2. Colour and symbolism: faction colours, Wu Xing element colours, seals, calligraphy, scroll and pattern motifs

### Takeaway
Two colour systems dominate: the Wei blue / Shu green / Wu red convention, which Koei Tecmo's own artists say was formalised by Dynasty Warriors, and TW3K's five-element (Wu Xing) scheme that ties hero class, attributes, buildings and even sound to one of five colours. Symbolic props that recur in praised games are the red seal, plum blossom, silk and scroll framing, gilded type and museum-sourced artefacts.

### Cited Findings
- Koei Tecmo CG veteran Ryota Kamei said the faction colours (Shu green, Wei blue, Wu red) were formally set as easy-to-read image colours by the Dynasty Warriors series. Colleague Yusaku Kimura recalled being told to paint Liu Bei in a red robe but make "一番中側の肌着を緑" (the innermost garment green). — [4Gamer, 10 Dec 2025, page 2](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/index_2.html)
- In early RTK portraits there was no per-officer rule for background colour; the goal was "顔の肌色が映える対比を生む" (a contrast that makes skin tones stand out). From RTK12 onward each officer has a designed background. — [4Gamer, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/)
- TW3K: "Wu Xing, which is this ancient philosophy of the five elements, underpins everything in the game" (Wojs). "Military, for example, is fire"; Vanguard-class heroes are linked to fire; lead designer Attila Mohacsi describes "supporting branches where the wood element feeds fire." Wojs calls the layer "not really in your face." — [TheSixthAxis, 25 Jan 2019](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2019/01/25/interview-how-total-war-three-kingdoms-blends-character-relationships-with-grand-strategy/)
- The five TW3K attributes are colour-coded to the elements: Earth yellow, Metal purple, Water blue, Wood green, Fire red. — [Total War Wiki (fan wiki, snippet-level)](https://totalwar.fandom.com/wiki/Wu_Xing)
- A Chinese review confirms the reading in play: green marks wood-element heroes such as Guan Yu and Dian Wei. — [少数派, 6 Jun 2019](https://sspai.com/post/55115)
- The Wu Xing scheme extends to audio: Creative Assembly built modular sound kits for Earth, Fire, Metal, Water and Wood, so spending an elemental skill point plays that element's sound. — [Game Developer, 2 Jul 2019](https://gamedeveloper.com/audio/behind-the-audio-of-i-total-war-three-kingdoms-i-)
- TW3K symbolic motifs: crimson 「三国」 seal on the main menu; ink plum blossom for reforms. — [ifanr, 12 Jun 2019](https://www.ifanr.com/app/1218987)
- 三国志·战略版's 2026 3D refresh gives each faction its own animated environment behind the hero: Shu misty mountains with birds and drifting cloud; Wu sunlight over the Yangtze with flowing water; Wei falling snow; Qun (independents) barren hills with blowing yellow sand; Han Luoyang pavilions with shifting light. A faction flag sways in the wind and takes about one sixth of the page, hiding on non-detail screens. Previously the faction backgrounds reused one template with only the flag colour changed. — [小米游戏中心, slg刀笔吏, 28 Aug 2026](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1279764826_1787911389955_16)
- 三国望神州 uses silk and painting-mount framing, classical pattern borders and gilded ("烫金") lettering when officers are promoted or unlock skills. — [小米游戏中心, 27 Oct 2025](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1585024444_1761544204648_100)
- 率土之滨's tenth-anniversary collaboration with the Palace Museum rebuilt in-game visual symbols around red walls and yellow tiles and added two museum paintings (《洛神赋图》 and 《关羽擒将图》) as in-game portrait items; an eleventh-anniversary collaboration with Gansu Provincial Museum (from 22 Oct 2026) added a "马踏飞燕" (Flying Horse of Gansu) city skin. — [17173, Oct 2025](https://news.17173.com/content/10142025/151801649.shtml); [17173, Oct 2026](https://news.17173.com/content/10162026/103212961.shtml) (both snippet-level)
- Koei Tecmo keeps reference sheets that sort decoration and mood by region and rank; for example Jiangnan officers show more skin to reflect the warm climate, and long drooping moustaches are used as a deliberate "Chinese" sign. — [4Gamer, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/)

### Inferences
- The full TW3K class mapping (Commander earth/yellow, Champion wood/green, Sentinel metal/purple, Strategist water/blue, Vanguard fire/red) is consistent with the three partial sources above but I did not open a page that lists all five class pairings; treat the per-class table as probable, not confirmed.
- A five-colour system works because it is reused everywhere a player looks: portrait frame, attribute bar, skill icon, unit card, building branch and sound. A solo project can copy the principle with five CSS custom properties (for example `--wx-earth`, `--wx-wood`) and a single lookup from officer archetype to token.
- Koei's "innermost garment green" detail shows faction colour working as an accent rather than a flood: a trim, sash, tassel or banner is enough. This is easy to request in AI portrait prompts and to reinforce with a coloured frame, banner or backdrop in CSS.
- Per-faction animated environments behind a static portrait (战略版) are a high-value, low-cost idea: one looping canvas or shader layer per faction (snow, mist, river glints, dust, palace light) placed behind a cut-out portrait.
- Browser approximation, seals: an SVG seal (red rounded square, seal-script or bold characters knocked out), eroded with `feTurbulence` and `feComposite`, stamped in with a quick scale-down, slight rotation and an opacity mottle. Use it for confirmations, city capture, treaties and save slots.
- Browser approximation, patterns: cloud (祥云), wave (海水江崖) and thunder-fret (回纹) motifs as tiling SVG backgrounds at low opacity; bronze and jade via layered CSS gradients plus a noise overlay; gold foil via `background-clip: text` with an animated linear gradient.
- Needs professionals: nothing here strictly does, but museum-grade motif accuracy (correct Han-era patterns rather than generic "oriental" ornament) needs reference research; the 率土之滨 museum tie-ins show that authenticity is now a selling point.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed bamboo-slip (竹简) UI motifs in a specific 2019–2026 title, although they are common in the genre.
- No developer explanation was found for the colour choices in 率土之滨, 三国：谋定天下 or 鸿图之下.
- Whether TW3K's metal element is shown as purple in every UI context relies on a fan wiki summary.

## 3. Hero portraits: static, layered, animated or 3D; expressions, framing and rarity treatment

### Takeaway
Koei Tecmo's portraits stay iconic because the house rule is "the face is everything": officers are painted as characters rather than historical reconstructions, about nine-tenths invented, with background colour chosen to flatter skin tone and heavy retake discipline. Chinese mobile strategy games have moved from flat cards to layered scenes (separate text, hero and animated faction background) and, in 2026, to full 3D hero models, while small indie teams still rely on static art plus scripted scene animation.

### Cited Findings

**Koei Tecmo (RTK series through RTK14 2020 and RTK8R 2024)**
- "キャラクターはやっぱり，顔が命ですから" (for a character, the face is everything): Kamei, who attributes to honorary chair Keiko Erikawa the rule that the background exists to set off the face. — [4Gamer, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/)
- Kimura: the policy is "武将は人物ではなくキャラクターとして描こう" (draw officers as characters, not as persons); references need not be followed too closely ("資料通りすぎなくてもいい"), and he agreed with the interviewer that roughly 90% is invention because Three Kingdoms-era sources are thin. — [4Gamer, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/)
- Framing history: bust-up through RTK11; waist-up from RTK12, drawn with A4 print and art books in mind; RTK12 and RTK13 used two images per officer (an intense one for battle, a calm one for domestic affairs); RTK14 returned to a neutral standing pose to suit conversation scenes. — [4Gamer, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/)
- Retakes: an RTK13 draft drawn front-on was sent back with the note to show more violent movement, such as Guan Yu on horseback; some officers were redrawn about 20 times because their character did not come through. — [4Gamer, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/)
- Character consistency over decades: Cao Cao's look was largely fixed from RTK IX; Kimura's more heroic RTK13 version broke the tradition of "fearsomeness" and was reverted for RTK14. RTK VII switched the whole series to a watercolour touch. The house reference was the pocket-size 中国劇画 三国志 picture books. — [4Gamer, page 2](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/index_2.html)
- Composition is allowed to break rules for eccentric figures: Zuo Ci was composed with a fish as the focal point, which "would not work for Zhao Yun or Zhou Yu"; Wutugu's first draft was "a bit tame" and his expression was pushed until he looked inhuman. — [4Gamer, page 2](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/index_2.html)
- Kamei on team make-up: Chinese designers "最初からリアルにものを見て描きます" (start from realism), while Japanese designers add realism to a manga and anime base; leads then even out the differences for unity. — [4Gamer, page 2](https://www.4gamer.net/games/952/G095211/20251121021/index_2.html)
- RTK8R raised the roster from about 600 to 1,000 officers. — [Gematsu, Oct 2023](https://www.gematsu.com/2023/10/romance-of-the-three-kingdoms-8-remake-details-new-features-enhancements)
- RTK8R's director wanted to avoid faces identical to RTK14 ("『14』と同じ顔になるのは可能な限り避けたくて"); many portraits were drawn new and many were selected from the mobile title 100万人の三國志, choosing the best of several versions. — [電撃オンライン, 4 Oct 2024](https://dengekionline.com/article/202410/20028); partly contradicted by Echigoya's earlier statement "過去のリソースはほぼ使っていません" (we use almost no past resources) in [Famitsu, 21 Sep 2023](https://www.famitsu.com/news/202309/21317855.html)
- Reception: RTK8R's "hand-drawn artwork is top notch" but characters appear as "static portraits". — [3rd-strike, 1 Nov 2024](https://3rd-strike.com/?p=169327). The PC version lets players insert their own portraits. — [Siliconera, 23 Oct 2024](https://siliconera.com/?p=1059785)
- List ordering as presentation: in RTK8R officer selection, famous officers are shown first ("有名な武将ほど上に表示"). — [Famitsu, 28 Sep 2024](https://www.famitsu.com/article/202409/19191)

**Total War: Three Kingdoms (2019)**
- Portraits are painted over the ink base so the figure seems to emerge from ink ("this painterly effect"). — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/how-creative-assembly-conceived-the-art-for-total-war-three-kingdoms/)
- Official dev diaries state that character portraits were drawn in Photoshop and take about eight days each on average, and that for Dong Zhuo the concept artist adjusted the 2D face to match the finished 3D model before a UI artist exported it into templates. — [Total War blog: Eight Princes, Eight Portraits](https://totalwar.com/?p=7501); [Designing Dong Zhuo](https://www.totalwar.com/?p=8457) (both snippet-level; pages were rate-limited)
- Chinese reception: portraits capture temperament (Cao Cao "刚毅脸庞配上阴鸷眼神", a resolute face with brooding eyes) without flattering or demeaning anyone. — [ifanr](https://www.ifanr.com/app/1218987). They draw on the 1994 CCTV series and John Woo's Red Cliff, which matches Chinese players' mental image; but outside the main cast "不少名人全是同一张龙套脸" (many famous figures share one generic extra's face), naming Sun Shangxiang, Diaochan and Guo Jia. — [少数派](https://sspai.com/post/55115)

**Chinese mobile and indie titles**
- 三国志·战略版's 2026 refresh separates text, hero and background into layers with the background animated; the gacha reveal brings layers in sequentially: faction, then general, then text introduction, then attribute panel. Players can switch back to the original 2D art, including ink-wash style hero art (about 1 GB download). — [小米游戏中心, 28 Aug 2026](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1279764826_1787911389955_16)
- 率土之滨 has redrawn many well-known officers in several rounds since 2021 using historical sources, and its hero list formally distinguishes "动态" (animated) from "静态" (static) portraits. — [17173](https://news.17173.com/z/stzb/content/10092022/150717873.shtml); [萌娘百科](https://zh.moegirl.org.cn/%E7%8E%87%E5%9C%9F%E4%B9%8B%E6%BB%A8) (both snippet-level; exact 17173 article not opened)
- 三国志：王道天下 (2026) advertises 3D models for every top-rarity officer, viewable through 360 degrees with dedicated animation and voice. — [机核 promotional submission, 18 Mar 2026](https://www.gcores.com/articles/212140)
- 三国望神州 portraits follow Han dress: civil officials in long cap and wide-sleeved cross-collar robe (Xun Yu), military officers in Han lamellar armour with visible scale pattern (Li Dian). — [小米游戏中心, 27 Oct 2025](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1585024444_1761544204648_100)
- A small indie team (三国：十三州风云, 2026) reported moving from "几个框" (a few boxes) to bespoke portraits fused with each officer's special skill, and added a full-scene animation for a mode switch made only of background, portrait, typewriter dialogue and fades. — [TapTap dev post, Apr 2026](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/793832209650814643)
- Production cost of animated 2D portraits: Spine-style rigs need the illustration split into many layers with hidden areas repainted by hand, and hair or ribbon motion is slow to key; the Spine developers say physics is generally handled at runtime. — [CSDN article](https://gitcode.csdn.net/69bc8e170a2f6a37c598b9b8.html); [Esoteric Software forum](https://ko.esotericsoftware.com/forum/d/15253-spine飘带头发有没有解算器) (snippet-level)
- A 2026 Creative Bloq feature profiles concept designer Xianglong, who "blends traditional Chinese ink aesthetics with digital painting in Photoshop" for Three Kingdoms characters. — [Creative Bloq, 16 Sep 2026](https://www.creativebloq.com/art/digital-art/this-artist-blends-traditional-chinese-ink-aesthetics-with-digital-painting-in-photoshop-to-create-dynamic-three-kingdoms-characters) (body text not retrievable)

### Inferences
- What makes Koei portraits read as iconic, per their own artists: face-first lighting and contrast, a readable archetype (heroic, fearsome, eccentric), long-term consistency of signature props (Guan Yu's beard and blade, Zhang Liao's helmet), and pose matched to context (calm for dialogue, violent for battle). These are art-direction rules that can be applied to AI-painted portraits through prompt discipline and curation, including Koei-style retakes until the character reads.
- TW3K's "generic extra's face" criticism is the main risk for an AI-painted roster of about 38: same-face syndrome. Vary age, face shape, facial hair, headgear and expression deliberately, and give each officer one signature prop.
- Two-pose sets (calm and battle) like RTK12 and RTK13 are a realistic stretch goal: generate a second image per major officer and use it for duels and battle cut-ins.
- Browser approximation, layered portrait: remove the background from each JPG (AI matting) to get a cut-out, then stack: animated faction backdrop, portrait, foreground particles, text. Add pointer or scroll parallax with CSS transforms.
- Browser approximation, pseudo-animated portrait: render the portrait on a subdivided plane in three.js with an AI-estimated depth map for 2.5D parallax; add masked sine or noise displacement for breathing (chest), hair and cloth sway, and a slow light sweep. Blink requires a second "eyes closed" frame (AI inpainting) cross-faded for about 120 ms. This gives perhaps 60 to 70% of the Live2D impression.
- Browser approximation, rank and rarity framing: frames built from CSS (ink ring for common, bronze, jade, gold-lacquer for higher ranks), element-colour trims, and a small seal with the officer's title; in lists use a tight face crop, in detail screens a waist-up crop with the faction environment behind, following Koei's framing logic.
- Needs professionals: true Live2D or Spine rigs with repainted hidden layers, consistent multi-expression sets, and 3D hero models that match 2D portraits (TW3K had to repaint a 2D face to match the 3D model). AI can produce variants but identity drift between expressions is a known weakness, so limit expression sets to a few key officers and review them carefully.

- No developer account was found of how 率土之滨 or 三国：谋定天下 animate hero art (tooling, layer counts, cost).

### Inferences
- The shared grammar of a "big moment" across these games: (1) isolate the subject (ring of soldiers, dimmed background, portrait cut-in); (2) change medium or register (2D to 3D, colour to ink, real time to slow motion); (3) title it (calligraphic name card or seal); (4) punctuate with one percussive sound.
- RTK8R shows that duel and debate can share one system and layout and still feel distinct through art (weapon clash versus rhetorical gesture), though reviewers noticed the sameness. Distinct palettes and effects per mode (steel sparks and red ink for duels; scrolls, brush strokes and blue ink for debates) would address that.
- Browser approximation, duel intro: two portrait cut-ins sliding from opposite sides inside diagonal `clip-path` panels, names in vertical calligraphy, an ink splash behind each, a "VS"-equivalent seal, then cut to the existing three.js mounted duel. During the 3D duel, add hit-stop (set animation time scale to zero for 60 to 120 ms), brief slow motion on decisive blows, camera shake, desaturation of the background with a red accent, and a brush-stroke slash sprite on impact. Have nearby troop sprites or instanced figures form a ring.
- Browser approximation, recruitment: copy the 战略版 sequence (faction banner drops, portrait fades up through ink, name written stroke by stroke, stats panel last), with a seal stamp to confirm.
- Browser approximation, city capture and coronation: animate faction colour spreading across the territory as an ink wash from the city outward (distance field plus noise threshold in the map shader), drop a banner, stamp a seal. For coronation, unroll a horizontal scroll (animated `clip-path` with roller ends), reveal a painted still with slow pan, gold-foil title, drums.
- Browser approximation, deaths: fade the portrait from colour to ink monochrome (CSS `grayscale` and `contrast` over paper with multiply), drift petals or leaves on canvas, show a vertical couplet or epitaph (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`), single bell or guqin note.
- Browser approximation, event stills: AI-painted stills in a consistent 小人书 or ink style, presented with slow pan and zoom, typewriter text and fades, as the indie example in section 3 does.
- Needs professionals: motion-captured wushu choreography and bespoke per-officer skill animations. Procedural IK and a small set of well-timed generic moves plus camera work are the realistic ceiling for a solo project.

## 5. Typography: combining Chinese and Latin type; calligraphic display versus readable body text

### Takeaway
Praised games reserve brush calligraphy for a few high-impact places (logo, chapter and boss titles, promotions) and keep body text plain and readable; the best practice described by NetEase's UI team is to write display lettering with a real brush and to match script style to content. A custom calligraphic typeface with alternate glyph forms, as in Black Myth: Wukong, is the high end.

### Cited Findings
- Black Myth: Wukong (2024) uses HANYI BlackMyth, a custom running-script (行楷) typeface by Hanyi Fonts with sharp strokes modelled on Ouyang Xun, offering multiple forms of the same character (rare in calligraphy fonts); it appears on chapter title cards and boss-name screens. — [Fonts In Use, entry Jan 2025](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/64872/black-myth-wukong-video-game-1)
- NetEase Thunderfire's UI team (忘川风华录) on calligraphic design: lettering may vary on regular, running, cursive, clerical and seal scripts but must stay legible ("字体的设计可不能天马行空"); match script to content (regular or seal for solemn text, running-cursive for bold and free text); decide whether text is the visual centre or a supporting element; fonts must fit the game world or they look jarring when used widely. — [机核, 网易雷火艺术中心, 14 Jul 2022](https://www.gcores.com/articles/153626)
- Same source on process: plan the composition first, then write with a real brush rather than warping fonts in software, which blurs pixels and loses precision; a strict grid of columns and rows is most readable for heavy UI use, while free-flowing columns suit secondary flourish text and are weak as titles; the first character sets the weight for the whole line; keep a continuous "行气线" (axis of energy) through the line; balance ink density. — [机核, 14 Jul 2022](https://www.gcores.com/articles/153626)
- 三国望神州 (2025) uses gilded lettering for promotions and skill unlocks. — [小米游戏中心](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1585024444_1761544204648_100)
- A TW3K player thread asked for the UI font to be replaced with an Eastern Han calligraphy-style face to strengthen the art style. — [Steam Workshop discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/1809691812/1742265965885733808?l=danish) (snippet-level)
- A calligrapher's fan project for Black Myth argues for hand-brushed lettering instead of stock computer fonts and font images. — [ArtStation, ikken-art](https://ikken-art.artstation.com/projects/XndBva) (snippet-level; page returned 403)

### Inferences
- A workable hierarchy for a bilingual browser game: (1) hand-brushed or AI-assisted calligraphy rendered as SVG or PNG for the logo, faction names and a handful of single-character stamps (勝, 敗, 斬, 降); (2) a calligraphic webfont for officer names and screen titles; (3) a Song/Ming serif for narrative text; (4) a clean sans for numbers and dense tables. Pair Latin with a restrained serif (old-style or transitional) for titles and a humanist sans for data; avoid "chop-suey" faux-Asian Latin fonts.
- Freely licensed candidates to evaluate (availability and licences should be checked before use): Ma Shan Zheng, Zhi Mang Xing, Liu Jian Mao Cao and Long Cang for brush display; Noto Serif SC (思源宋体) and LXGW WenKai for body; Noto Sans SC for data. These names are from my own knowledge, not from the sources above.
- Browser approximation: Chinese webfonts are large, so subset display fonts to the exact glyphs used (officer names, titles) and load body fonts in `unicode-range` slices. Use `writing-mode: vertical-rl` with `text-orientation: upright` for name plates, couplets and scroll text. Animate titles with a brush-shaped mask sweep so they appear written.
- Calligraphy for proper nouns only is the safe rule: running or cursive scripts for body text would hurt readability, which the NetEase team explicitly warns about.
- Needs professionals: a bespoke calligraphic typeface. Single brushed words and names can be commissioned cheaply or produced from open fonts with manual clean-up.

### Gaps
- No source identified the Latin typefaces in TW3K, RTK8R or Black Myth: Wukong, or described how these games pair Chinese and Latin type in the UI.
- No published typographic guideline was found for Koei Tecmo's UI.

## 6. Sound-and-vision pairing that sells the era (brief)

### Takeaway
TW3K's audio team deliberately quietened the UI to match the ink visuals, replacing metallic "cinematic" hits with traditional instrument recordings and abstract ink-like sounds, and extended the five-element colour code into five sound kits. Native-language voice is repeatedly cited as an authenticity signal.

### Cited Findings
- Earlier Total War UIs used sword scrapes and anvil hits; for TW3K the goal was UI sound that is "clean, minimal and refined", built from original recordings of traditional Chinese instruments layered with abstract, non-literal sounds representing the heavy ink effect. — [Game Developer, Tom McCaren and Valentin Goellner, 2 Jul 2019](https://gamedeveloper.com/audio/behind-the-audio-of-i-total-war-three-kingdoms-i-)
- Modular kits for Earth, Fire, Metal, Water and Wood add element-specific sweeteners to actions. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/audio/behind-the-audio-of-i-total-war-three-kingdoms-i-)
- About 3,000 lines of ambient crowd voice were "performed by native Mandarin speakers, something which we felt was important for authenticity", triggered by season, settlement type, time of day and public order. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/audio/behind-the-audio-of-i-total-war-three-kingdoms-i-)
- Chinese reviewers praised TW3K's Chinese-style score and Mandarin narration and cinematics voiced in clear standard Chinese. — [少数派](https://sspai.com/post/55115); [ifanr](https://www.ifanr.com/app/1218987)
- RTK8R: the remake's re-orchestrated soundtrack is preferred by one reviewer and both old and new tracks are selectable; two reviewers criticised the Japanese-only voices as odd for a story set in China. — [Siliconera](https://siliconera.com/?p=1059785); [3rd-strike](https://3rd-strike.com/?p=169327)

### Inferences
- Browser approximation: a small Web Audio sample set covers most of the effect. Suggested mapping: soft brush or paper swish for panel open and close; a single plucked guqin or guzheng note for hover and confirm; a wooden "thock" for seal stamps, synchronised to the frame where the seal reaches full scale; a large war drum (大鼓) hit for duel start, turn end and city capture; a bell or chime for deaths and season change; five short sweeteners keyed to the Wu Xing colours. Keep UI sounds quiet and dry, as TW3K did.
- Synchronisation matters more than fidelity: trigger the sound from the same timeline as the animation (for example the Web Animations API `finished` promise or a shared clock) so the stamp and the thud land together.
- Needs professionals: an original score. Licensed or public-domain traditional instrument recordings are a reasonable substitute.

### Inferences
- The clearest lesson for a browser game with existing ink-and-gold theming: cohesion and restraint win praise; density and inconsistency draw complaints. Priorities, in order: one material language everywhere; fewer, larger, illustrated screens instead of icon grids; a strict cap on badges and notifications; and portrait variety.
- Strong 2D art next to weaker 3D is noticed (RTK8R). Where the project's 3D duel models cannot match the painted portraits, keep the camera wide or stylise the 3D (ink outline, flat shading, silhouette against a painted arena) and put the detailed face in a 2D cut-in.
- Static portraits are accepted when the art is excellent but are called a letdown in a full-price 2024 release; light motion (parallax, breathing, weather behind the figure) is cheap insurance.
- Western reviewers reward readability (pinned tooltips, zoom, clear friend and foe colours) as much as beauty; an ink aesthetic must not reduce contrast on data. Keep numbers and controls on plain paper areas with dark ink text.
